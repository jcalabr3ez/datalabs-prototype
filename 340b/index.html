Hospital charity care

Charity-care cost was 2.71 percent of hospital total costs on the 2023 CMS Provider Cost Report PUF, the public Worksheet S-10 file behind RAND TL-303. Participating 340B hospitals (matched on CCN) filed at 2.85 percent of total costs; other hospitals filed at 2.43 percent. The ranking is the state share, not dollars.

Legislative mapping

31,133 unique active contract pharmacies are assigned to 4,017 state house districts (2024 boundaries) by Census ZCTA land-area majority. 1,304 contract rows had a ZIP with no 2020 ZCTA in that file. A ZIP can cross district lines. Filter the table by state.

▶What this page does not cover

Does not reconstruct a historical stock of entities that later left the program. Does not geocode street addresses. A ZIP can cross district lines; assignment uses the ZCTA's majority land-area district. Does not cover advice to a hospital or manufacturer, or predictions of federal rulemaking.
