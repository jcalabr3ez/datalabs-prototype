# Pioneer DataLabs: internal beta

Live site: [https://datalabsai.netlify.app](https://datalabsai.netlify.app)

This catalog is ready for Pioneer staff to try. It is not a public launch.
Thirty-one applications are live. Patents by State is in build. Every figure
cites a primary source. The ask box may only answer from those ledgers.

## What to do (about 15 minutes)

1. Open the front door. Confirm the masthead says DataLabs Beta.
2. Ask one question you would actually use in a brief. Starters that already
   have a published number:
   - How many new business applications were filed in the United States last month?
   - What is the unemployment rate in Massachusetts?
   - How much does a Massachusetts household pay for a kilowatthour?
   - Is the T back to pre-pandemic ridership?
   - Which state gained the most people from domestic migration?
3. Follow the answer onto the tool page. The large number and Figure 1 should
   match the question. Click a state on a fifty-state map if there is one.
4. Open two tools from your vertical in the catalog. Check the source register
   and the how-to-cite line in the footer.
5. Write [jcalabrese@pioneerinstitute.org](mailto:jcalabrese@pioneerinstitute.org)
   if something is wrong.

## What to report

Include the tool name, the figure and where it appears, the vintage on the
page, and the source you believe is correct, with a link if you have one.
Also write if:

- the ask box declines a question a live page already answers
- the public question and the hero number do not match
- a page is hard to read on a laptop or a phone
- a name, place, or vintage is wrong

Questions typed in the ask box are logged on the site so the next tool can
come from demand. Individual questions are not published.

## What this is not

- Not advice. Not a filing, purchasing, or legal tool.
- Not a download portal. There are no spreadsheets to take away.
- Not complete coverage of every Pioneer Tableau workbook. Patents by State
  is still in build. A page that names an exclusion is telling the truth.
- Not a live feed. Ledgers refresh on a published cadence. An older vintage
  on a catalog row is often the latest official file, not an error. Status:
  [datalabsai.netlify.app/status](https://datalabsai.netlify.app/status/).

## How to cite a figure in a draft

Application name, Pioneer Institute DataLabs, the vintage in the masthead,
and the source id next to the figure. Example: Pioneer Institute DataLabs,
Retail Electricity Prices, data through Dec 31, 2024 (SRC-401).
