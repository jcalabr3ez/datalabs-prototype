Enrollment, spending, and MCAS

Fall enrollment fell from 63,738 in 1993-94 to 44,416 in 2025-26 (SRC-634-01). Total expenditures per pupil rose from $18,831 in FY 2009 to $34,833 in FY 2025 (SRC-634-02). On the Next Generation MCAS, 31% of Boston grades 3-8 students met or exceeded in ELA in 2017 and 29% in 2025. Math was 31% in 2017 and 28% in 2025. The 2020 test was not administered (SRC-634-04).

▶What this page does not cover

Does not cover charter or Horace Mann districts that are not 00350000, waitlists, lottery outcomes, statewide Massachusetts enrollment or statewide MCAS (those sit on Massachusetts Schools), City of Boston payroll or the BPS budget line on Boston City Payroll, other cities, or advice about a school. Next Generation MCAS was not administered in 2020; that year is a gap, not a zero. BPS does not publish a machine-readable route roster; later School Committee memos that say daily buses were fewer than 625 do not replace the last exact published count.
