What are the key takeaways?

City of Boston earnings totaled $2.46 billion in calendar year 2025 across 25,397 employees (SRC-627-01). Boston Police Department was the largest department at $534.48 million (SRC-627-01). The FY26 adopted operating appropriation was $4.91 billion (SRC-627-02). Boston Public Schools was the largest department at $1.66 billion (SRC-627-02).
