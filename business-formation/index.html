What are the key takeaways?

Seasonally adjusted business applications were 578,926 in Jul 2026, +23.4% from a year earlier (SRC-613-01). Massachusetts filed 7,497, rank 25 of 51 (derived, SRC-613-01). BLS Business Employment Dynamics put the Massachusetts private-sector establishment birth rate at 3.9% in 2025 Q4 (8,540 establishments, SRC-613-02). Deaths are published through 2025 Q1, when the death rate was 2.6% (5,722 establishments, SRC-613-02). In the last overlapping quarter, 2025 Q1, the birth rate was 2.9% and the death rate was 2.6% (SRC-613-02).
