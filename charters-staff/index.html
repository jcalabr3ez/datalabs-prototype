What are the key takeaways?

  1. Massachusetts public charter enrollment was 47,993 in 2022-23, rank 22 of 46 (derived, SRC-609-01).
  2. Public schools employed 76,978 full-time-equivalent teachers in Massachusetts in Fall 2022, rank 13 of 51 (derived, SRC-609-02).
  3. U.S. charter enrollment was 3,717,857 (SRC-609-01).

Public charter-school enrollment was 3,717,857 in 2022-23 (SRC-609-01). Massachusetts enrolled 47,993, rank 22 of 46 states with a published count (derived, SRC-609-01). Public schools employed 3,228,895 full-time-equivalent teachers in Fall 2022 (SRC-609-02). Massachusetts employed 76,978, rank 13 of 51 (derived, SRC-609-02).
