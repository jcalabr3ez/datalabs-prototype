What are the key takeaways?

  1. The Massachusetts imprisonment rate was 96 per 100,000 residents at year-end 2023, rank 50 of 50 (derived, SRC-631-03).
  2. Sentenced admissions were 1,641 (SRC-631-03).
  3. Prisoners age 17 or younger in adult prisons numbered 0 (SRC-631-03).

State and federal correctional authorities held 1,254,224 prisoners at year-end 2023 (SRC-631-02). Massachusetts held 6,002, rank 36 of 50 (derived, SRC-631-02). The imprisonment rate was 360.0 per 100,000 U.S. residents at year-end 2023; Massachusetts was 96.0, rank 50 of 50 (derived, SRC-631-03). Sentenced admissions were 1,641 in Massachusetts (SRC-631-03). Prisoners age 17 or younger in adult prisons numbered 0 in Massachusetts (SRC-631-03). That is youth in adult prisons, not the juvenile-justice custody count. FBI crime rates and IC3 reports are not a stable machine-readable state file on this page.
