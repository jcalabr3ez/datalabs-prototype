What are the key takeaways?

State and federal correctional authorities held 1,254,224 prisoners at year-end 2023 (SRC-631-02). Massachusetts held 6,002, rank 36 of 50 (derived, SRC-631-02). FBI crime rates, juvenile incarceration, and internet-crime reports are pending on this page.
