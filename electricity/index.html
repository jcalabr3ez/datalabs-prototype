▶What this page does not cover

This tool publishes the all-sector average retail price by state, the residential, commercial, and industrial averages from the same EIA-861 file, plus sales, generation, capacity, and per-capita figures where the population join is complete. It does not publish utility or city rates, forecasts, bill calculators, or rate-case advice. The U.S. figure is always EIA’s U.S. Total row. Questions on excluded subjects asked through the DataLabs front door are declined rather than improvised.

About this tool. Retail Electricity Prices is a Pioneer Institute DataLabs research tool, published and versioned. It is the successor to the Retail-Price Electricity dashboard previously hosted on US DataLabs. Corrections and data refreshes are logged in the public changelog. It is a living data tool, not a static report.
