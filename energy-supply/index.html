What are the key takeaways?

Energy-related carbon dioxide emissions were 4,780.7 million metric tons in 2024 (SRC-624-01). Massachusetts emitted 58.1 million metric tons, rank 30 of 51 (derived, SRC-624-01). Total energy consumption was 94,543,438 billion Btu in the United States in 2024 (SRC-624-02). Massachusetts consumed 1,289,504 billion Btu, rank 27 of 51 (derived, SRC-624-02). SEDS total energy production was 103,447,522 billion Btu in the United States in 2024 (SRC-624-03). Massachusetts produced 58,773 billion Btu, rank 46 of 51 (derived, SRC-624-03).
