What are the key takeaways?

Energy-related carbon dioxide emissions were 4,934.4 million metric tons in 2022 (SRC-624-01). Massachusetts emitted 59.2 million metric tons, rank 32 of 51 (derived, SRC-624-01). Total energy consumption was 94,543,438 billion Btu in the United States in 2024 (SRC-624-02). Massachusetts consumed 1,289,504 billion Btu, rank 27 of 51 (derived, SRC-624-02). SEDS production remains pending because that file was not in the consumption extract.
