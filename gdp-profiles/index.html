What are the key takeaways?

Real GDP was $23.85 trillion in the United States in 2025, chained 2017 dollars (SRC-615-01). Massachusetts was $644.18 billion, rank 12 of 51 (derived, SRC-615-01). Personal income was $26.11 trillion in 2025 (SRC-615-02). Massachusetts was $697.21 billion, rank 13 of 51 (derived, SRC-615-02). Per capita personal income was $76,393 in the United States and $97,456 in Massachusetts, rank 3 of 51 (derived, SRC-615-02). Current-dollar GDP was $30.76 trillion in 2025 (SRC-615-03). Massachusetts was $820.10 billion (SRC-615-03). Manufacturing was $62.80 billion and finance and insurance $71.33 billion (SRC-615-03).
