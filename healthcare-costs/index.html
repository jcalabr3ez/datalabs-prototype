Out-of-pocket as a share of income

MHIS does not publish an average dollar out-of-pocket cost. It publishes the share of family income spent out of pocket. 18.5 percent of residents were in families that spent 5 percent or more of income (derived, SRC-633-02).

Coverage and high-deductible plans

97.9 percent of residents were insured at the time of the survey. 46.4 percent of the privately insured had a high-deductible plan (at least $1,400 single or $2,800 family). Among residents with a behavioral health visit, 17.2 percent paid entirely out of pocket.
Source: CHIA 2025 MHIS tables B.1-1, B.3-1, and G.2-1 (SRC-633-02).
