Fall enrollment in degree-granting postsecondary institutions was 18,580,026 in 2022 (SRC-608-01). Massachusetts enrolled 468,960, rank 12 of 51 (derived, SRC-608-01). The SAT mean total score for 2023 graduates was 1028 in the United States, with 51.2% of graduates taking the test (SRC-608-02). Massachusetts scored 1112 (57.0% taking the test) (SRC-608-02). Degree-granting institutions employed 1,530,513 faculty in Fall 2023 (SRC-608-03). State faculty counts are not in that national table. The IPEDS 6-year bachelor's graduation rate for the 2017 entry cohort was 64.5% at all 4-year institutions (SRC-608-04). That Digest table is national; it has no state column. A state-level faculty table is not in the current Digest xlsx set. Full-time faculty numbered 859,825 in Fall 2023; 192,300 were professors and 48.8% were women (SRC-608-05). That Digest table is national and has no state column.
