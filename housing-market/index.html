What are the key takeaways?

Permit-issuing places authorized 721,052 housing units in the United States through Jun 2026, -1.3% from the same months of 2025 (derived, SRC-616-01). Massachusetts authorized 7,148, rank 30 of 51 (derived, SRC-616-01). The FHFA all-transactions house-price index rose 3.65% in Massachusetts in 2025, rank 21 of 51 on annual change (derived, SRC-616-02). New Jersey had the largest increase at 6.16% (SRC-616-02). The S&P/CoreLogic Case-Shiller Boston index was 356.29 in 2026-05 (+2.7% from a year earlier) (SRC-616-03). Boston is the only Massachusetts city in that series.
