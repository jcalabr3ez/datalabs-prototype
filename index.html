About

What DataLabs is

Pioneer Institute's catalog of public-data applications, aligned to our four core research verticals. The goal is to curate novel data tools for the public, policymakers, and anyone interested.

Research and educational use

This site is provided strictly for research and educational purposes. It does not constitute financial, legal, insurance, tax, actuarial, or investment advice, it creates no professional or advisory relationship, and it should not be relied upon for any coverage, purchasing, filing, financial, or legal decision. Consult a qualified professional before acting on any matter addressed here.

Figures are compiled in good faith from public records issued by third parties and are accurate to the stated vintage at the time of verification. Those bodies publish on their own schedules and may revise prior figures without notice. Pages compiled here state their known limits. Partner catalog rows may be older, use a different method, or have moved; the freshness dot is the cue.

Corrections

If a figure compiled here is wrong, write datalabs@pioneerinstitute.org. Please include the tool name, the figure and where it appears, the vintage shown on the page, and the source you believe is correct, with a link if you have one. Partner-site errors should go to the site that publishes that page; we still welcome a note if a catalog link is broken or mis-titled.
