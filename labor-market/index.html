What are the key takeaways?

Massachusetts seasonally adjusted unemployment was 4.4 percent in Jun 2026, rank 18 of 51 when states are ordered from highest rate to lowest (derived, SRC-614-01). District of Columbia was highest at 6.0 percent; South Dakota was lowest at 2.0 percent (SRC-614-01). Average weekly wages were $1,569 in the United States in 2025 Q4 (derived, SRC-614-02). Massachusetts was $1,980, rank 2 of 51 (derived, SRC-614-02). UI initial claims were 187,408 in the United States in the week ending 2026-08-08 (derived, SRC-614-03). Massachusetts filed 4,617 initial claims and 75,706 continued weeks (SRC-614-03). A CPS demographic extract is not in this ledger.
