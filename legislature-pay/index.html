▶What this page does not cover

Does not cover House or Senate staff, municipal pay, other state jobs a member may hold, employer-paid GIC health or MSERS pension contributions (those are not named-employee lines on this file), the statutory office-expense allowance (not a named column here), calendar 2026 year-to-date as the headline, or advice about a member. Department-level Commonwealth payroll sits on Massachusetts State Payroll.
