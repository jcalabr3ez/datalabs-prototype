Massachusetts state tax collections were $11.62 billion in the first quarter of 2026, +5.2% from the same quarter of 2025 (SRC-628-01). The United States (excluding D.C.) collected $393.07 billion (SRC-628-01). Individual income was the largest Massachusetts source after the total. Individual income taxes were 50.4% of Massachusetts 2026 Q1 collections ($5.85 billion, SRC-628-01). On the annual Census STC file, Massachusetts collected $41.34 billion in FY 2023, rank 8 of 50 (derived, SRC-628-02). DOR monthly reports and tax credits remain pending.
