What are the key takeaways?

Massachusetts was 7,154,084 on July 1, 2025 (SRC-625-01). The largest of 351 cities and towns was Boston city at 672,973; the smallest was Gosnold town at 62 (SRC-625-01). A DLS levy file is not posted as a stable public CSV. Population peers are the five Census 2025 municipalities closest in resident count (SRC-625-02). Boston's nearest peer is Worcester city at 213,862 (derived, SRC-625-02). This is not the old Pioneer socioeconomic peer workbook. A DLS levy file is not posted as a stable public CSV.
