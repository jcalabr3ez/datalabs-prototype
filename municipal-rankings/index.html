What are the key takeaways?

From 2020 to 2025 the largest population gain among Massachusetts cities and towns was Amherst Town city at 13,117; the largest loss was Revere city at -1,768 (derived, SRC-626-01). DLS debt, levy, revenue, and municipal crime files are not posted as stable public CSVs. On DESE FY 2025 total expenditures per pupil, Truro was highest at $59,111 among 389 districts (SRC-626-02). DLS debt, levy, revenue, and municipal crime files are not posted as stable public CSVs on this pass.
