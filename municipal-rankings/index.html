What are the key takeaways?

From 2020 to 2024 the largest population gain among Massachusetts cities and towns was Amherst Town city at 13,183; the largest loss was Boston city at -2,064 (derived, SRC-626-01). Crime, debt, education, spending, and tax rankings are pending because those DLS and DESE files were not reachable this pass.
