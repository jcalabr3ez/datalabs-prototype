▶What this page does not cover

Does not cover the per-town profile stock (Massachusetts Town Profiles), Boston payroll, or advice about a town meeting warrant. DLS debt, levy, revenue, tax, and municipal crime rankings are not a stable public CSV: decline those.

It replaces these Tableau workbooks: Rankings-Crime, Rankings-Debt, Rankings-Education, Rankings-Expenditures, Rankings-Financial Strength, Rankings-Revenues, Rankings-Taxes.
