From 2020 to 2025 the largest population gain among Massachusetts cities and towns was Amherst at 13,117; the largest loss was Revere at -1,768 (derived, SRC-626-01). DLS debt, levy, revenue, and municipal crime files are not posted as stable public CSVs. On DESE FY 2025 total expenditures per pupil, Truro was highest at $59,111 among 389 districts (SRC-626-02). DLS debt, levy, revenue, and municipal crime files are not posted as stable public CSVs on this pass. Among towns with an ACS 2020-2024 median household income, Carlisle was highest at $250,001 (ACS top-code for $250,000 or more) (SRC-626-03). Monroe had the highest poverty rate at 38.5% (SRC-626-03). Chilmark had the highest median home value (SRC-626-03). DLS debt, levy, revenue, tax, and municipal crime files are not stable public CSVs.

▶What this page does not cover

Does not cover the per-town atlas stock (Municipal Atlas), Boston payroll, or advice about a town meeting warrant. DLS debt, levy, revenue, tax, and municipal crime rankings are not a stable public CSV: decline those.

It replaces these Tableau workbooks: Rankings-Crime, Rankings-Debt, Rankings-Education, Rankings-Expenditures, Rankings-Financial Strength, Rankings-Revenues, Rankings-Taxes.
