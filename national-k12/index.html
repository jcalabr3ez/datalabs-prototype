What are the key takeaways?

Public elementary and secondary enrollment was 49,387,403 in the United States in Fall 2024 (SRC-607-02). Massachusetts enrolled 915,932, rank 17 of 51 (derived, SRC-607-02). The public high school 4-year graduation rate was 86.6% in 2021-22 (SRC-607-03). Massachusetts was 90.1%, rank 5 of 48 (derived, SRC-607-03). Out-of-school suspensions reached 1.32% of U.S. public-school students in 2020-21; Massachusetts was 0.59% (SRC-607-04). On the 2024 NAEP, grade-4 reading averaged 214.3 in the national public sample; Massachusetts scored 224.8, rank 1 of 51 (SRC-607-05). Grade-4 math was 237.3 nationally and 246.5 in Massachusetts (SRC-607-05). Grade-8 reading was 256.7 / 268.0; grade-8 math was 272.2 / 283.5 (SRC-607-05).
