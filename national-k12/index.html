What are the key takeaways?

  1. On the 2024 NAEP, Massachusetts ranked 1 of 51 in grade-4 reading (scale score 224.8) and grade-8 reading (268.0) (SRC-607-05).
  2. From 2019 to 2024, national public grade-4 reading changed −5.2 points; 2 of 51 states rose. Louisiana gained the most at +6.1. Massachusetts was −6.3, rank 34 of 51 (SRC-607-05).
  3. Massachusetts spent $27,008 per pupil in FY 2024, rank 5 of 51 (derived, SRC-607-06).

Public elementary and secondary enrollment was 49,387,403 in the United States in Fall 2024 (SRC-607-02). Massachusetts enrolled 915,932, rank 17 of 51 (derived, SRC-607-02). The public high school 4-year graduation rate was 86.6% in 2021-22 (SRC-607-03). Massachusetts was 90.1%, rank 5 of 48 (derived, SRC-607-03). Out-of-school suspensions reached 1.32% of U.S. public-school students in 2020-21; Massachusetts was 0.59% (SRC-607-04). On the 2024 NAEP, grade-4 reading averaged 214.3 in the national public sample; Massachusetts scored 224.8, rank 1 of 51 (SRC-607-05). Grade-4 math was 237.3 nationally and 246.5 in Massachusetts (SRC-607-05). Grade-8 reading was 256.7 / 268.0; grade-8 math was 272.2 / 283.5 (SRC-607-05). From 2019 to 2024, national public grade-4 reading changed -5.2 points; 2 of 51 states rose and 49 fell (SRC-607-05). Louisiana had the largest grade-4 reading gain at +6.1 (SRC-607-05). Grade-8 math changed -8.8 nationally; 0 states rose (SRC-607-05). NCES NPEFS current expenditures per pupil were $17,644 in the United States in FY 2024 (SRC-607-06). Massachusetts was $27,008, rank 5 of 51 (derived, SRC-607-06).
