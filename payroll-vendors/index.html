What are the key takeaways?

Commonwealth payroll totaled $10.89 billion in calendar 2025 across 162,473 employee rows (SRC-630-01). UNIVERSITY OF MASSACHUSETTS SYSTEM (UMS) was the largest department at $1.82 billion (SRC-630-01). Comptroller-recorded spending was $98.33 billion in fiscal 2025 (SRC-630-02). Quasi-public payroll totaled $352.80 million in calendar 2025 across 3,238 rows (SRC-630-03). MASSPORT was the largest quasi-public employer (SRC-630-03). The largest non-payroll vendor in FY 2025 was UMS - SUMMARY TRUST PAYMENT at $2.40 billion (SRC-630-04).
