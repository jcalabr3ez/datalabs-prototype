What are the key takeaways?

The Census vintage 2025 estimate put the United States at 341,784,857 on July 1, 2025 (SRC-617-01). Massachusetts was 7,154,084, with domestic migration of -33,340, rank 47 of 51 (derived, SRC-617-01). Under the 2023 USDA rural-urban continuum codes, 98.5% of Massachusetts 2020 county population lived in metro (RUCC 1-3) counties (SRC-617-02). The state has 11 metro counties and 3 nonmetro counties (SRC-617-02).
