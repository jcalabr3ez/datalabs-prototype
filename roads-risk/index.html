What are the key takeaways?

Annual vehicle-miles of travel were 3,294,031.2 million in 2024 (SRC-623-01). Massachusetts recorded 62,013.3 million, rank 19 of 50 (derived, SRC-623-01). OpenFEMA Public Assistance records show $4.22 billion in federal share obligated to Massachusetts (SRC-623-03). The National Risk Index mean county score is 78.8, rank 7 of 51 (derived, SRC-623-04).
