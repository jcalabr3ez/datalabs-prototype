What are the key takeaways?

Adjusted gross income on individual returns was $14.78 trillion in tax year 2022, from 159,651,330 returns (SRC-621-01). Massachusetts AGI was $422.21 billion on 3,567,790 returns, rank 9 of 51 (derived, SRC-621-01). Among Massachusetts counties, Middlesex County had the most adjusted gross income in tax year 2022 at $125.60 billion (derived, SRC-621-02). A dedicated AGI-percentile-by-state file is not posted; size-of-AGI stubs are stored under derived.secondary.
