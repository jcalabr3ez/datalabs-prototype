What are the key takeaways?

Adjusted gross income on individual returns was $14.78 trillion in tax year 2022, from 159,651,330 returns (SRC-621-01). Massachusetts AGI was $422.21 billion on 3,567,790 returns, rank 9 of 51 (derived, SRC-621-01).
