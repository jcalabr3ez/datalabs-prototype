What are the key takeaways?

Massachusetts had a net domestic taxpayer flow of -15,378 returns in tax years 2022-23 (83,296 in, 98,674 out), rank 47 of 51 (derived, SRC-620-01). Texas had the largest net inflow; California the largest net outflow (SRC-620-01). Among Massachusetts counties, Bristol County had the largest net domestic taxpayer inflow (168 returns) and Middlesex County the largest net outflow (-4,549) in 2022-23 (derived, SRC-620-02).
