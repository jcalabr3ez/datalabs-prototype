What are the key takeaways?

U.S. transit agencies reported 690,656,393 unlinked passenger trips in June 2026 (SRC-622-01). The MBTA reported 26,288,869, rank 5 of 518 agencies (derived, SRC-622-01). MTA New York City Transit was the largest agency (SRC-622-01). In NTD report year 2024, U.S. agencies reported $65.62 billion in operating expenses, with a 17.2% farebox recovery rate (derived, SRC-622-02). The MBTA spent $2.16 billion, recovered 19.2% from fares, and cost $8.59 per unlinked trip (derived, SRC-622-02).
