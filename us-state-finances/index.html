What are the key takeaways?

State government tax collections were $393.07 billion in the first quarter of 2026, +5.7% from a year earlier, excluding D.C. (SRC-629-01). Massachusetts collected $11.62 billion, rank 8 of 50 (derived, SRC-629-01). Rainy-day funds and public-employee counts are pending.
