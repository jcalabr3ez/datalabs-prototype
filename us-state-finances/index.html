What are the key takeaways?

State government tax collections were $393.07 billion in the first quarter of 2026, +5.7% from a year earlier, excluding D.C. (SRC-629-01). Massachusetts collected $11.62 billion, rank 8 of 50 (derived, SRC-629-01). State governments employed 4,441,091 full-time-equivalent workers in 2023; Massachusetts employed 105,348, rank 14 of 50 (derived, SRC-629-03). Individual income taxes were 47.8% of Massachusetts FY 2023 state tax collections, rank 5 of 44 (derived, SRC-629-04). NASBO rainy-day figures remain pending.
